FAQ

How much does a turnkey website cost in Astana?

A landing page starts at $350, a business website of up to 7 pages at $700, an online store at $1,300. The exact price depends on pages and features; I give a quote after a short talk.

How long does it take?

A landing page takes 5–7 working days, a business website 2–3 weeks, an online store 3–5 weeks, counted from when texts and photos are ready.

Can I pay in tenge?

Yes. Prices are in US dollars for reference; you can pay in tenge at the rate on the invoice date.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The domain and hosting are registered in your name, and I hand over all access after launch.

Can I order just a logo or design without a website?

Yes. Graphic and interface design can be ordered separately: a logo from $150, brand identity from $450, app design from $900. If you need a website later, it will match the same style.

Do you only work in Astana?

No, I work remotely with clients across Kazakhstan and abroad. Calls via Telegram, WhatsApp or Google Meet.
