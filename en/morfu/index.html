A / DescriptionWho it’s for

Morfu lives in the menu bar. Drop a video, photo, PDF, SVG or document on its icon, get the format you need and see how much space you saved. Everything stays local; drag the result out or paste it from the clipboard.

Who it’s forPeople who want a one-drop converter without extra windows.

C / How it works03 steps

How it works

  1. 01

    Download the zip, move Morfu.app to Applications, first launch — right-click → Open.

  2. 02

    Install the tools with one command: brew install ffmpeg webp mupdf-tools potrace pngquant.

  3. 03

    Drop files on the “m” or press ⌘V in the panel. Morfu keeps itself up to date.

D / FAQE / Requirements

FAQ

How is Morfu different from Morfus?

Morfu is a Swift menu bar panel. Morfus is the next generation in Rust: a round menu at the cursor and many more actions.

Does it need the internet?

No. Even speech-to-TXT/SRT uses the built-in macOS recogniser.

Can it compress a screenshot in the clipboard?

Yes: ⌃⌥C converts the clipboard contents and puts them back.

Requirements

  • 01 macOS 13 Ventura or later
  • 02 Homebrew with ffmpeg, webp, mupdf-tools, potrace, pngquant
  • 03 Media Engine on Apple Silicon makes video 2–3× faster
